Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

This page (askforbrycekc.com) is a personal site maintained by Bryce Hill, a Sales Consultant at Gary Crossley Ford. This policy explains what information is collected when you visit, how it's used, and your choices.

Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, this site uses a web analytics service (Statcounter) to understand traffic and how visitors use the page. This may collect:

  • Pages viewed and time spent on the site
  • Referring website or search terms that led you here
  • Approximate location, based on IP address
  • Browser type, device type, and general technical information

This information is aggregated and used only to understand overall site traffic. It is not used to identify you personally.

Information You Provide

If you submit the contact form on this site, the information you enter (such as your name, phone number, email, and message) is sent directly to Bryce Hill and/or Gary Crossley Ford staff to follow up with you about your inquiry. This information is not sold, and is not used for any purpose other than responding to your request.

Cookies

This site may use basic cookies or similar technology solely to support the analytics described above. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used.

Third-Party Links

This site links out to third-party sites, including Gary Crossley Ford's inventory pages, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, and DealerRater. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which govern any information you provide there.

Your Choices

You are never required to submit the contact form to browse this site or view inventory. Most browsers also let you block or clear cookies through your browser settings.
